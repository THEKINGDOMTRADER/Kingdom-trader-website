Please read this

Expected amounts are targets, not guarantees. If results fall short, you receive less than the target. You can also get back less than you invested.

Your money is managed together with other members' funds. This keeps minimums low. It does not make profits more likely or protect your capital.

Targets can be ambitious. A return of 30% or more in a month is exceptional and should never be treated as dependable. The targets shown here are illustrations.

Plan terms · draft

Trade-for-me plan terms

Draft for the prototype. These terms must be reviewed by a lawyer before any real deposit is accepted.

  1. What the plan is. You choose a plan length and an amount. The Kingdom Trader trades funds on your behalf.
  2. Targets are not guarantees. The expected amount shown is a target. Trading carries a high risk of loss, and you can receive back less than you put in.
  3. Funds are managed together. Your money is managed together with other members' funds in one portfolio. Results are shared in proportion to each member's contribution.
  4. Our fee. If the portfolio earns more than the combined targets, The Kingdom Trader keeps the amount above the targets as its fee.
  5. If results fall short. If the portfolio ends below the targets, members receive what the portfolio actually holds, in proportion to their contributions, even when that is less than the target or the amount invested.
  6. Statements. You can view and print a statement for every completed plan.
  7. Prototype. This page uses virtual funds. No real money is accepted yet.
